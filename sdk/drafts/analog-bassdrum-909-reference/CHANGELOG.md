# Analog BD pending release notes

## 0.1.4-experimental — candidate, 9 October 2026

- Match the 909 attack more closely to the recorded instrument: a short negative transient replaces the delayed positive click, with a rounder body and a less exaggerated pitch sweep at minimum Tune.
- Add subtle differences in attack strength and noise between consecutive hits, while preserving sequencer timing and pitch.
- Many thanks to **Skee Mask**, who kindly recorded his TR-909 so we could match Analog BD more closely.
- The 808 engine, saved controls and existing TDEP/SAT smoothing are preserved. This is an unreleased candidate: physical persistence, worst-case chip timing and maximum FX-load qualification remain pending.

Copy these version-matched notes to `src/community/module-changelogs.json` when the candidate is qualified and the catalogue version advances. The public changelog currently credits Skee Mask in a clearly marked development note; its 0.1.3 behaviour and previous history are retained.
